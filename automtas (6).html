How an engagement runs

Four stages, one team, from first sensor to full rollout.

Each stage has a clear owner and a clear output — no separate "strategy" team handing off to a separate "build" team.

01 / Discover
Frame the problem
We map your data sources, device constraints, and the decision you actually need the system to make — before any model gets built.
02 / Prototype
Prove it on real data
A working model and a working device talking to each other, tested against your data — not a slide deck.
03 / Deploy
Ship to the field
Edge inference, device provisioning, and the pipelines that keep models fresh once they leave the lab.
04 / Scale
Run the fleet
Monitoring, retraining, and fleet management so accuracy holds at 10 devices and at 10,000.
